About the forecasts

Current departures use the B2 flight-number baseline, based on historical airline and flight-number outcomes. Weather and aircraft rotations are not used in live forecasts. Estimates are provisional until a complete schedule is saved and published during the 10:30–11:00 PM Eastern window. Historical dates use saved B4v2 backtest predictions. Reconstructed forecasts are excluded from accuracy scoring. Check your airline for current flight status.
