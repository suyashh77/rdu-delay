ID: EXP-003
Date: 2026-10-25
Idea ID (backlog): F2 (Overnight aircraft position)
Hypothesis: Adding overnight aircraft positioning features from day D-1 (destination of yesterday's final leg, whether finished before 23:00 ET cutoff, arrival delay if finished, and overnight scheduled turnaround slack) to `LGBM_WX` improves prediction skill on morning departures (leg 1) and overall fleet Brier and CRPS without violating calibration or coverage guardrails.
Change: New model `LGBM_FULL` (src/rdu/models/flight_level.py). Concatenates 8 point-in-time overnight position features from `data/features/position.parquet` (built by `src/rdu/features/position.py`) alongside rotation, turnaround buffers, baseline history, and 77 forecast weather features.
Features added:
  - `prev_day_tail_seen`: 1 if tail flew on day D-1, 0 otherwise
  - `prev_day_dest_is_rdu`: 1 if yesterday's last scheduled destination was RDU, 0 if elsewhere
  - `prev_day_completed_before_cutoff`: 1 if yesterday's last leg finished before 23:00 ET
  - `prev_day_last_arr_delay`: actual arrival delay (min, capped 180) if completed before cutoff (NaN if still airborne)
  - `prev_day_ended_late15`: 1 if completed before cutoff with arr_delay >= 15 min
  - `prev_day_ended_late45`: 1 if completed before cutoff with arr_delay >= 45 min
  - `prev_day_overnight_turn_min`: scheduled turn minutes between yesterday's last arrival and today's departure
  - `prev_day_n_legs`: number of legs the airframe flew on day D-1
Point-in-time verification: When `completed_before_cutoff == 0` (still airborne at 23:00 ET), delay metrics are strictly NaN (no actual outcome peek); verified by `test_position_features_respect_cutoff`.
Champion compared against: `EXP-002-fast` (`LGBM_WX`)
Expected effect: Brier skill +1% to +2% over EXP-002; largest gain on morning departures (Leg 1, hours 05–08).
Mode: fast screen (every 8th day, 114 days, 2023-01..2025-06, monthly refits)
Runtime / cost estimate: ~45–60 min fast screen. Free data (BTS national chains).

## Result
### Run 1 (EXP-003-fast) — LGBM_FULL with weather + overnight positioning
Fast screen, every 8th day (114 days, 37,087 flights, 2023-01-01 to 2025-06-30), refit every 28 days:
- Overall metrics:
  - Brier: **0.1366** (vs EXP-002: 0.1371, vs EXP-001b: 0.1419, vs B4v2: 0.1531)
  - CRPS: **11.338** (vs EXP-002: 11.428, vs EXP-001b: 12.018, vs B4v2: 13.29)
  - Log loss: **0.6821** (vs EXP-002: 0.6865, vs EXP-001b: 0.7130, vs B4v2: 0.7560)
  - ECE: **0.00786** (further drop from 0.0093; 47% lower than B4v2's 0.0148)
  - PIT Coverage80: **80.12%** (within [0.75, 0.85] guardrail)
  - Naive Coverage80: **0.9062**
- Comparison vs EXP-002-fast (on identical 37,087 flights):
  - Brier skill: **+0.37%** (Brier diff: -0.00050, 95% CI [-0.00103, -0.00005], excludes zero)
  - CRPS skill: **+0.79%** (CRPS diff: -0.0897 min, 95% CI [-0.1460, -0.0483], excludes zero)
  - ECE change: -0.00144 (calibration improved)
  - Log loss change: -0.00440 (improved)
- Comparison vs champion B4v2-full (on identical 37,087 flights):
  - Brier skill: **+10.79%** (Brier diff: -0.01652, 95% CI [-0.01882, -0.01463], excludes zero)
  - CRPS skill: **+14.67%** (CRPS diff: -1.9494 min, 95% CI [-2.2224, -1.7444], excludes zero)
- Slice performance (Hypothesis Confirmed):
  - Early Morning (Hours 05–08): CRPS dropped from `7.963` down to **`7.570 min`** (-0.393 min error reduction; Brier improved to 0.0873).
  - Leg 1 Flights: CRPS dropped from `8.428` down to **`8.185 min`** (-0.243 min error reduction; Brier improved to 0.0969).
  - Morning ECE dropped to a remarkable **0.0042**.
- Decision: **Kept**. Passes all acceptance criteria in fast screening mode with statistically significant gains on Brier and CRPS, driving targeted improvements on the early morning Leg 1 wave.
