ID: EXP-002
Date: 2026-10-25
Idea ID (backlog): F8 (TAF), F10 (NBM), F11 (SPC Day 2), F12 (snow/ice)
Hypothesis: Adding forecast-weather features issued before the 22:30 ET cutoff (TAF wind/vis/ceil/thunder, NBM station thunder/precip probabilities, day-worst values, and SPC Day 2 convective risk) to the flight-level LightGBM model beats EXP-001b on Brier score (bootstrap 95% CI excludes zero) without worsening CRPS, ECE, or interval coverage, with largest gains on calm-vs-storm days and afternoon flights.
Change: New model `LGBM_WX` (src/rdu/models/flight_level.py). Extends `LGBMBuckets` by concatenating 77 point-in-time forecast weather features from `data/features/weather.parquet` (built by `src/rdu/features/weather.py`). Features include:
  - TAF at scheduled times at RDU, other airport, and (for departures) inbound origin: wind, gust, visibility, ceiling, thunder/snow/freezing bitmasks, tempo wind/vis/thunder.
  - NBM hourly station forecasts: 3-hour thunder prob (t03), wind speed, gust, ceiling, visibility, prob of snow (psn), freezing rain (pzr), sleet (ppl), temp.
  - Day-worst values at RDU and destination/origin: max t03, max gust, min vis, min ceiling, max psn, max pzr.
  - SPC Day 2 convective outlook categorical risk (0 to 5) at RDU and destination/origin.
  - All weather features frozen at 22:30 ET (rdu.ingest.weather); point-in-time verified by `check_weather_issue_time` (0 flights late).
Champion compared against: EXP-001b-fast (and champion-000 B4v2-full)
Expected effect: Brier skill +1 to +3% over EXP-001b; largest gain on storm days (SPC Day 2 >= SLGT) and afternoon/evening departure banks.
Mode: fast screen (every 8th day, 114 days, 2023-01..2025-06, monthly refits) -> full
Runtime / cost estimate: ~45–60 min fast screen. Free data (IEM archive).

## Result

  - Naive Coverage80: **0.9043**
- Comparison vs EXP-001b-fast (on identical 37,087 flights):
  - Brier skill: **+3.37%** (Brier diff: -0.00479, 95% CI [-0.00688, -0.00303], excludes zero)
